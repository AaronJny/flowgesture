# FlowGesture

[English](README.md) | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

Make your mouse and trackpad work your way.

FlowGesture is a native macOS app for custom gestures, smoother scrolling and everyday shortcuts. Your rules stay on your Mac unless you choose to sync them.

## What you can do

- **Draw your shortcuts.** Use directional gestures or record your own shapes, with a visible mouse trail.
- **Make every button useful.** Assign mouse side buttons, single/double/triple clicks and keyboard shortcuts.
- **Scroll your way.** Smooth mouse-wheel scrolling, adjust its speed and reverse its direction independently of your trackpad.
- **Customize touch gestures.** Map multi-finger taps, swipes and pinches, with separate rules for trackpads and touch mice.
- **Work across apps.** Use global or app-specific rules, modifier keys and screen corners.
- **Choose the action.** Run shortcuts or AppleScript, open apps/files/URLs, select menu commands, arrange windows and control media.
- **Keep your setup.** Import/export rules, restore backups or sync through a folder you choose. Switch between English and Chinese in Settings.

## Get started

1. Download and extract the archive, then move `FlowGesture.app` to Applications.
2. Open the app and choose **FlowGesture Settings** from the menu bar.
3. Grant **Accessibility** and **Input Monitoring** in System Settings → Privacy & Security, then refresh the permission status.
4. Choose **Add examples** to try a setup, or **Add rule** to create your own. Enable FlowGesture when you are ready.

You can pause or quit from the menu bar at any time. AppleScript actions that control another app may also request **Automation** permission.
